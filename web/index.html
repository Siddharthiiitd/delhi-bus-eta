How to read this

The big number is the estimate for the chosen departure time. "Usually" is the same trip at the quietest hour in the data. The range is the typical spread; it assumes delays on neighbouring stretches go together, so it is wide on purpose. Confidence is low where the model had little data and fell back to the timetable.
